04

Mathematical proof

BUY · 3% FEE
f = ⌊3A / 100⌋
m = ⌊(A − f) × 1018 / P⌋
P × m ≤ A × 1018
R′ = R + A   ·   S′ = S + m
P′ = ⌊R′ × 1018 / S′⌋ ≥ P
SELL · 6% FEE
g = ⌊x × P / 1018⌋
q = g − ⌊6g / 100⌋
q × 1018 ≤ x × P
R′ = R − q   ·   S′ = S − x
P′ = ⌊R′ × 1018 / S′⌋ ≥ P
The inequalities follow the exact integer-floor operations in the current FIT contract.
