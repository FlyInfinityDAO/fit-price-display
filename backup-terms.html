Backups are automated on a best-effort basis. Successful scheduling does not guarantee that every resource can be exported or restored. Provider outages, permission changes, API limits, configuration errors, and changes to provider services may interrupt or limit a backup. You are responsible for checking backup completion and periodically verifying that backup files can be restored.

Backup files are intended to be stored in the Google Drive destination you authorize. GitHub, Cloudflare, and Google operate their own services under their respective terms and privacy policies.
