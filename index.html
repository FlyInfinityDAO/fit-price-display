How the FIT price is calculated

The displayed on-chain price is calculated as the DAI reserve held by the FIT token contract divided by the current FIT total supply. This is a contract formula, not a promise of future market value or guaranteed returns.

FIT Price = DAI Reserve ÷ FIT Total Supply

A connected platform

Network, FIT, Gift and DAO are presented as connected parts of the Fly Infinity platform. Each component has a distinct role while contributing to the wider ecosystem.

NETWORK↔FIT↔GIFT↔DAO
